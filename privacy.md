# Privacy Policy for LEX Word Practice

**Effective Date:** June 14, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 14, 2026

Thank you for using LEX Word Practice ("the App"). Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not handle.

## Summary

**LEX Word Practice does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** All of your study data stays on your device or in your own private iCloud account.

## Information We Do Not Collect

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit any of the following to us or any third party:

- Your name, email address, or contact information
- Account credentials or login information
- Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics data
- Crash reports, usage statistics, or telemetry
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data

The App does **not** use any third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or analytics services.

## Information Stored on Your Device and in Your iCloud

The App stores the following information locally on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, syncs it through your own private iCloud account:

- Your study progress, including words attempted, correctness, and review schedule
- Your deck selections, filters, and app preferences
- Your study session history

This information is stored under **your own Apple ID** in your private iCloud container. We do not have access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If you disable iCloud or sign out of your Apple ID, data will remain only on your local device.

## Subscriptions and Purchases

LEX Word Practice offers in-app subscriptions and purchases handled entirely through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. We do not process, receive, or store your payment information. All purchase data is handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [App Store Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/).

## Children's Privacy

LEX Word Practice is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal information, there is no data for us to access, modify, or delete on your behalf. You can delete all data stored by the App by deleting the App from your device and, if applicable, removing its data from iCloud in your device's Settings.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Material changes will also be communicated through the App.
